There is a often a delay between when a song is streamed (i.e. the sale) and when DSP’s report the sale to distributors, and therefore when Tone finally receives it. If you download your Sales Excel Statement, the ‘sales’ column will show you an approximate breakdown of when the sales were reported to the label.
